Minesweeper

Controls

  • Reveal: click or tap a cell.
  • Flag: turn on Flag mode and click, press F while pointing at a cell, Shift+click, right-click / two-finger click, or long press on touch.
  • Open neighbors (chord): click, double-click or Shift+click a number whose flags are all placed, or press Space while pointing at it.
  • Keyboard: press Tab to enter the board, arrow keys move, Enter/Space reveal, F flags, R restarts.
