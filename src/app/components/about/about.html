Play Minesweeper online, free

Minesweeper Duel is the classic logic puzzle in your browser. Open every safe cell without hitting a mine. The numbers tell you how many mines touch each cell, so careful thinking beats luck. No download, no account, and it works on a laptop, a tablet or a phone.

How to play Minesweeper

  • Click a cell to open it. Your first click is always safe.
  • A number shows how many of the eight cells around it hide a mine.
  • Flag a cell when you are sure it is a mine. The counter shows how many are left.
  • When a number has all its flags, click it to open the rest of its neighbors at once.
  • Open every safe cell to win. Beginner has 10 mines, Intermediate 40, Expert 99.

Minesweeper 1v1 against a friend or a bot

Race someone on exactly the same board. One of you creates a room and shares the five-letter code, and it works on any network. No friend around? Play against a bot with three strengths, from a casual player to a strong one. You choose whether a mine ends the round or adds a time penalty.

No-guessing Minesweeper

Turn on No guessing in the settings and every board can be solved by logic alone, checked by a solver before you play. You never lose to a coin flip. Stuck? The Hint button shows one move that logic proves is right.

Questions

Is it free?
Yes. There are no accounts, no payments and no ads.
Can I play on my phone?
Yes. Tap to open a cell, and long-press or use the Flag button to place a flag.
Can I play with the keyboard?
Yes. Move with the arrow keys or W A S D, open with Enter or Space, and flag with F.
Are my best times saved?
Yes, in your browser, with separate records for each difficulty and for no-guessing.
